## Owner Manual Page 3
**Content:** Fume and Gas Safety (inhalation hazards of welding/plasma cutting fumes, 6 rules) and Arc Ray Safety (eye and skin protection, 6 rules).

**Tables:** None.

**Diagrams & images:**
- Top right: two circular/triangular hazard icons next to the "Fume and Gas Safety" heading — a respirator/face-mask icon (wear respiratory protection) and a triangular warning icon depicting a figure with fumes (inhalation hazard).
- Next to "Arc Ray Safety" heading: a triangular warning icon depicting a welder/arc with radiating rays (arc ray hazard).

**Key facts:**
- Welding and plasma cutting produce toxic fumes; exposure can increase the risk of certain cancers (cancer of the larynx and lung cancer) and is linked to: early onset of Parkinson's Disease, heart disease, ulcers, damage to the reproductive organs, inflammation of the small intestine or stomach, kidney damage, and respiratory diseases such as emphysema, bronchitis, or pneumonia.
- Use natural or forced air ventilation and wear a respirator approved by NIOSH.
- Do not use near degreasing or painting operations.
- Work in a confined area only if well-ventilated or while wearing an air-supplied respirator.
- Follow OSHA guidelines for Permissible Exposure Limits (PEL's) and ACGIH (American Conference of Governmental Industrial Hygienists) Threshold Limit Values (TLV's).
- Arc ray protection: wear ANSI-approved welding eye protection featuring at least a number 10 shade lens rating.
- Wear leather leggings, fire resistant shoes or boots; no pants with cuffs, no shirts with open pockets; keep clothing free of grease, oil, solvents, or flammable substances; wear dry, insulating gloves.
- When welding/cutting overhead or in confined spaces, wear flame resistant ear plugs or ear muffs.

## Owner Manual Page 4
**Content:** Electrical Safety (10 rules, headed "ELECTRIC SHOCK can KILL") and Fire Safety (9 rules, headed "ARC AND SLAG can cause fire").

**Tables:** None.

**Diagrams & images:**
- Top right, next to "Electrical Safety" heading: triangular electric-shock warning icon (lightning bolt).
- Next to "Fire Safety" heading: triangular fire-hazard warning icon (flame).

**Key facts:**
- Turn off, disconnect power, and discharge electrode to ground before setting down torch/electrode holder and before service.
- Connect to a grounded, GFCI-protected power supply only.
- Do not use near water or damp objects; do not expose welders to rain or wet conditions; do not use outdoors.
- People with pacemakers should consult their physician(s) before use — electromagnetic fields near a heart pacemaker could cause pacemaker interference or failure.
- Insulate yourself from the workpiece and ground using nonflammable, dry insulating material (dry rubber mats, dry wood or plywood).
- "THIS WELDING MACHINE MUST BE CONNECTED TO POWER SOURCE IN ACCORDANCE WITH APPLICABLE ELECTRICAL CODES."
- Fire safety: remove or make safe all combustible materials for a radius of 35 feet (10 meters) around the work area.
- Keep an ABC-type fire extinguisher near the work area.
- If combustibles on the other side of a metal wall/ceiling cannot be moved, designate a fire watch equipped with a fire extinguisher during cutting and for at least one half hour after cutting is completed.
- Do not dispose of hot slag in containers holding combustible materials; vent closed containers (including castings) before preheating, welding, or cutting.

## Owner Manual Page 5
**Content:** Welder Use and Care (6 rules), Maintenance safety (4 rules), and Gas Shielded Welding - Cylinder Safety (7 rules), ending with "SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS."

**Tables:** None.

**Diagrams & images:**
- Next to "Gas Shielded Welding - Cylinder Safety" heading: a triangular warning icon depicting a pressurized gas cylinder (cylinder explosion hazard).
- Bottom of page: safety alert symbol (triangle with exclamation mark) beside the bold line "SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS."

**Key facts:**
- Do not use the welder if the switch does not turn it on and off; such a welder must be repaired.
- Disconnect the plug from the power source before making any adjustments, changing accessories, or storing the welder.
- Ensure the switch is in the off-position before connecting to power source or moving the welder.
- Do not use the welder for pipe thawing.
- Have the welder serviced by a qualified repair person using only identical replacement parts.
- Maintain labels and nameplates; if unreadable or missing, contact Harbor Freight Tools for a replacement.
- Unplug the welder from its electrical outlet before any inspection, maintenance, or cleaning procedures.
- Cylinder safety: cylinders can explode when damaged; do not weld on a pressurized or closed cylinder; keep cylinders away from any electrical circuits including welding circuits; keep the protective cap in place over the valve except when the cylinder is in use; protect cylinders from heat, being struck, physical damage, slag, flames, sparks, and arcs.
